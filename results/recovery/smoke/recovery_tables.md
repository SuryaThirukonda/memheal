# MemHeal recovery measurements

Generated by `measure.py recovery` from recover.py's records. Recovery is applied to a copy of each run's last pre-target snapshot and the target task family is replayed.

64 records; 8 runs; 64 poisoned records at checkpoint 0.

*ASR* = attacker outcomes over every replayed task. *BR* (Section 8.7) = mean fraction of tasks whose (tool, destination) matches the clean run. *BMP* (Section 8.6) = probes still answered correctly, among those the same run answered correctly with no recovery ('none'); runs with no 'none' record are left out of BMP and counted under *no none baseline*. *Collateral* = quarantined memories labelled benign, ambiguous or outside the poison lineage. *Root already consolidated* shows why `root` alone often changes nothing retrievable. Counts are means per run.
